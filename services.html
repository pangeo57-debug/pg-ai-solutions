How we work

Four steps, each one checked.

Before we move to the next step, we check that the previous one solves your real problem.

Understand

We learn who will use it, what limits you have and what the real goal is, before any design or code.

02

Design

We design the data, the logic and the screens together, so no expensive changes are needed later.
