Route optimization

RoutePal

Live

Plans a whole week of appointments and visits: who, when and in what order, using real travel times. Underneath runs a multi-phase optimization engine for routing with time windows (VRPTW).

9optimization phases
175automated tests
4languages
  • Weekly or single-day plans, built from each client's available slots and split shifts
  • Multi-phase engine: constraint backtracking, simulated annealing and large neighbourhood search (LNS)
  • Real distances and drive times (OSRM/HERE), with rush-hour traffic per appointment
  • Benchmarked on Solomon VRPTW instances against best published and exact (Held-Karp) optima
  • Group and paired visits, map with navigation, PDF export, installs on the phone

B2B marketplace

Surplo

In development · demo on request

A marketplace where small businesses sell surplus and unsold stock to other businesses, with payment held in escrow until the goods arrive.

  • Standing buy requests: the buyer says what they need, a seller fills it in one click
  • Offers and counter-offers, then escrow payment (card, Apple/Google Pay, PayPal, SEPA)
  • The seller is paid only when the buyer confirms delivery; disputes go to an admin
  • Business verification, reviews and an order flow with full history
  • Web app plus a native iOS app (Capacitor)
