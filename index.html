SELECTED WORK

Two products. Two very different problems.

One is a from-scratch optimization engine. The other is a fast, honest business website. Both were designed, built, and shipped end-to-end.

Flagship product

ROUTIQ — route & schedule optimization for mobile professionals

A progressive web app that solves what generic map apps ignore: optimizing a full day's route and schedule together for tutors, technicians, and anyone visiting multiple clients a day. The optimization core evolved through three engines — backtracking search, then simulated annealing, now an Adaptive Large Neighborhood Search (ALNS) — and is benchmarked against standard vehicle-routing test instances (Solomon, Augerat).

ABOUT THE STUDIO

Software with the rigor of the mathematics behind it.
