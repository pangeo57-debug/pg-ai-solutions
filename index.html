LIVEFlagship software · Route & appointment optimization

RoutePal

Plans a full week of appointments and home visits: who is seen when, in what order, on real road times. Under the hood is a multi-phase optimization engine for vehicle routing with time windows (VRPTW).

9
optimization phases
175

4
languages
  • VRPTW
  • Simulated annealing
  • LNS
  • PWA
Visit live ↗
  • Weekly schedules or a single day, built around every client’s availability windows and split shifts
  • Multi-phase engine: backtracking constraint search, simulated annealing and large neighborhood search (LNS)
  • Real road distances and times (OSRM/HERE), with rush-hour traffic applied per appointment
  • Benchmarked on Solomon’s VRPTW instances against published best-known solutions, and against exact optima (Held-Karp)
  • Group sessions and pairs, map with turn-by-turn navigation, PDF export, installable on the phone
