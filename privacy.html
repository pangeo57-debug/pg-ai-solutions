What this website collects, why, and your rights.

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This page explains what personal data this website collects, why, and what your rights are. It is written in plain language on purpose.

Who is responsible

PG AI Solutions, Patras, Greece. Email: pangeo57@yahoo.gr. Phone: +30 697 191 9078.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, your email address, your message, the investment range you select, and whether you are writing about a new project or a partnership. We only receive this if you choose to send the form.
  • Technical data: like any website, the company that hosts it receives basic technical information (such as your IP address and browser type) when a page is loaded. We do not use it to identify you.

What we do not do

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts and scripts are served from this website itself, not from third parties, so loading a page does not send your data to other companies.

Why we use your data

Only to answer your message and, if you ask for it, to discuss a project or a collaboration. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before a contract, and our legitimate interest in replying to people who contact us (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (f)).

Who receives it

  • A form-delivery service (formsubmit.co) that passes your message to our email inbox.
  • Our email provider, where the message is stored.
  • The company that hosts this website.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising. Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area.

How long we keep it

We keep your message for as long as needed to answer you and to work together if that happens. If no project or collaboration follows, we delete it within 12 months.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, limit how we use it, or stop using it, and you can ask for it in a portable format. Write to pangeo57@yahoo.gr. You also have the right to complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (www.dpa.gr).

Changes

If we change how the website works (for example by adding analytics), we will update this page before we do it.
